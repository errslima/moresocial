Remember the people who matter, and make plans with them.

Moresocial keeps a private, source-backed memory of your relationships and helps you organize gatherings and write messages you send yourself.

What you are asked to allow

  • Your Google name and email to sign you in.
  • Read your Gmail (last 90 days, up to 500 messages) to remember what people told you.
  • Read your primary Google Calendar (past and next 90 days) to spot conflicts when you plan.

Read-only: Moresocial never sends email, changes your calendar, or shares your data with other users. You can untick Gmail or Calendar on Google's screen; the rest of Moresocial still works. WhatsApp can be linked later with a QR code.
